Pay for parking by the minute.

ParkPay finds the parking area you are standing in, starts the clock with one tap and charges only for the time you used.

See how it worksFor cities and lot owners

How it works

  1. Arrive

    The app uses your phone’s location to find the parking area and shows its price right now.

  2. Start

    Pick your vehicle, choose how long you plan to stay, and tap Start parking.

  3. Get reminded

    You get a nudge before your time is up, and if you drive away without ending.

  4. End and pay

    Tap End. You pay for the exact minutes and get a receipt in the app.

For cities and lot owners

Draw an area, set a price

Mark a lot or a street on a map or satellite view at street zoom. Corners snap to neighbouring areas, sizes and edge lengths are measured as you draw, and shapes phone GPS cannot tell apart are flagged. Set rates by day and hour, publish, and drivers see it within seconds.

Enforce with a phone

Officers check a licence plate in the app and see at once whether the vehicle has a valid session.

See what is happening

Revenue per day and per area, who is parked now, refunds, tickets, and a log of every change.

Three apps, one system

ParkPay

For drivers. Find the area you are in, start and end parking, pay for the exact minutes.

ParkPay Enforce

For parking officers. Nearby paid vehicles, plate checks and tickets.

ParkPay Admin

For operators. Draw and edit areas on the phone, change rates, publish, see revenue and manage your team.

Web console

The same admin tools on a large screen, plus refunds, reports, CSV export and the audit log.

ParkPay is in pilot. Payments in the current test version are simulated: no money moves.
