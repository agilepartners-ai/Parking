Pay for parking by the minute.

ParkPay finds the parking area you are standing in, starts the clock with one tap and charges only for the time you used.

See how it worksFor cities and lot owners

How it works

  1. Arrive

    The app uses your phone’s location to find the parking area and shows its price right now.

  2. Start

    Pick your vehicle, choose how long you plan to stay, and tap Start parking.

  3. Get reminded

    You get a nudge before your time is up, and if you drive away without ending.

  4. End and pay

    Tap End. You pay for the exact minutes and get a receipt in the app.

For cities and lot owners

Draw an area, set a price

Mark a lot or a street on a map, set rates by day and hour, and publish. Drivers see it within seconds. No meters to install.

Enforce with a phone

Officers check a licence plate in the app and see at once whether the vehicle has a valid session.

See what is happening

Revenue per day and per area, who is parked now, refunds, tickets, and a log of every change.
